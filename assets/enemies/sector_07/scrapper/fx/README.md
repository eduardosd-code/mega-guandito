# Scrapper FX

Runtime atlases reconstructed from `assets/concept/SCRAPPER/FX/`:

- `impact_fx.png`: 4 frames at 64×64.
- `charge_trail_fx.png`: 5 frames at 96×64.
- `sparks_fx.png`: 4 frames at 64×64.

All three effects have real transparency and remain separate from the body sprite, hurtbox, and attack hitbox. Impact and sparks are connected to current gameplay events; charge trail is a reusable scene prepared for the alternate charge attack.
